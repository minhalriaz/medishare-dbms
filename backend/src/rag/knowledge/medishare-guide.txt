MediShare is a free medicine donation and distribution platform.

Donors can donate available medicines through the platform. Donated medicines can be reviewed and verified before they are made available for distribution.

Organizations can manage medicine inventory and track received and available quantities.

Medicine requests allow organizations or authorized users to request medicines based on their needs.

Medicine verification records contain information about the donation item, the person who performed the verification, the verification result, and verification remarks.

The MediShare system maintains information about medicines, donations, inventory, organizations, users, requests, and verification records.
